# Tao Book Publishing Pipeline - Product Brief

> Autonomous book publishing system for Tao of Founders content.
> Extends existing repo: `tao-growth-engine`

---

## Vision

A high-quality book publishing pipeline that packages existing and new Tao of Founders content into books at quarterly cadence, using AI for editing, formatting, imagery, and launch -- while constantly testing new products and categories within the founder psychology audience.

---

## Content Sources

### Already Available
- Substack posts (existing catalog, ready to compile)
- Research from academic pipeline (entrepreneurial psychology)
- Book highlights and notes (Readwise)
- Voice memo insights and observations

### To Create
- Canonical research mashup: all mental models, frameworks, key findings
- "What's shown to help, and by how much" -- evidence-based toolkit
- Entrepreneurial psychology coaching guides
- Method / framework / guide products

### Remix Opportunities
- Public domain classics revisited for entrepreneurial psychology lens
- Mashup of existing research across multiple fields
- Curated collections by theme

---

## Publishing Workflow

### Phase 1: Content Selection & Curation
- Select content theme / category
- Pull from available sources
- Identify gaps to fill
- Organize by logical chapters/sections

### Phase 2: Review & Editing
- AI-assisted review of content quality
- Ensure coherence across compiled chapters
- Edit for book format (vs. blog post format)
- Fact-check claims and citations

### Phase 3: Design & Formatting
- High-quality visuals (cover, interior graphics)
- Professional formatting for print + digital
- "Everything to a really high degree" -- quality bar is high

### Phase 4: Launch Preparation
- Amazon KDP setup
- Launch strategy design
- Cross-promotion with Substack
- Consider other platforms beyond Amazon

### Phase 5: Ship & Promote
- Publish to Amazon
- Promotional campaign
- Track sales, reviews, rankings

### Phase 6: Signal Collection
- What resonates? What's useful?
- Reviews and feedback analysis
- Feed signals back into content strategy

## Product Strategy

### Audience
- Entrepreneurs (always)
- Topic: founder psychology (always)

### Cadence
- **1 book every 3 months** (quarterly)

### Product Types to Test
| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Compiled posts | Substack posts organized by theme |
| Research toolkit | Mental models, frameworks, evidence-based methods |
| Coaching guide | How to coach yourself as a founder |
| Remixed classic | Public domain work reframed for founders |
| Visual guide | Heavy imagery, frameworks, diagrams |

### Tactical Approach
- Constantly test new categories and formats
- Always same audience (entrepreneurs)
- Always same topic (founder psychology)
- Find what actually interests and resonates
- Use signals to double down
